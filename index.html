A NEW CHAPTER
BEGINS

Vyuha is the AI & Machine Learning student association — a collective of curious minds who believe that the future of technology is built in classrooms, labs, and late-night coding sessions.

Born in 2026, Vyuha isn't about inheriting a legacy — it's about creating one. We are students who want to go beyond textbooks, explore the frontiers of artificial intelligence, and build things that matter.

This is where it starts. This is the beginning.
